# AL KAUSAR BAKREY — Complete Business Management Website (Master Prompt)

## Project Overview

Mujhe ek **complete web-based Business Management System** develop karna hai jiska naam **AL KAUSAR BAKREY** hoga.

Yeh website meri livestock/bakra business ko end-to-end manage karegi. Is website ka purpose yeh hai ke business ka har record ek hi platform par available ho aur real-time monitoring ho sake.

Website par har cheez ka complete record maintain ho:

* Kitna stock aaya
* Kitna stock gaya
* Kitni sale hui
* Kitna cash receive hua
* Kis cashier ne kitna collect kiya
* Kis employee ko salary di
* Kis supplier se purchase hui
* Kitna expense hua
* Kitna profit hua

Har module aapas mein linked ho taake reports aur dashboard automatically update hote rahen.

# 21. AI Development Rules (Strict)

The AI must NEVER:

* Assume any feature
* Remove any requested feature
* Rename modules
* Change workflow
* Change layout
* Skip any section
* Add any new functionality without approval
* Decide UI/UX itself
* Decide database structure itself

Instead:

1. Complete one module at a time.
2. Show the proposed implementation.
3. Wait for approval.
4. Only then continue to the next module.

The website must include **every feature listed above** without omission. If any requirement is unclear, stop and ask for confirmation before proceeding.
